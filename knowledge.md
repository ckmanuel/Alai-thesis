## Thesis (Velasquez)

- Author: Ahlai Jehziel M. Velasquez
- Degree: Bachelor of Arts in English Language Studies
- Institution: Pampanga State Agricultural University (PSAU), Magalang, Pampanga
- Title: Persuasive Language in Call-to-Action Phrases of Facebook Skincare Product Advertisements
- Brands studied: Luxe Organix, Olay, The Ordinary, Pond’s, Myra
- Timeframe of ads: January 2023 – June 2026
- Corpus size: 34 CTA phrases (one missing Pond’s slot for Jul–Dec 2024)
- Frameworks: Aristotle (ethos, pathos, logos) + Yule directive subtypes (command, order, request, suggestion) via Anggraini & Afriana (2023)
- Main findings (from manuscript): Logos dominant (64.71%), then Pathos (32.35%), Ethos rare (2.94%). Directive subtypes: Order 64.71%, Suggestion 35.29%; Command and Request absent. Most frequent combination: Logos + Order.
